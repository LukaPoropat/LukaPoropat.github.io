Publications

My astrometry is published by the IAU Minor Planet Center in Minor Planet Electronic Circulars (MPECs), which announce new near-Earth objects and comets and credit every observer and measurer by name, and in the Minor Planet Circulars. NASA ADS indexes the circulars with their observers as authors, so an up-to-date list of mine is on NASA ADS.
