Luka Poropat

Welcome! I am a final year MSc student at EFZG – Managerial Informatics. Astronomer and observing specialist at Višnjan Observatory in Istria, Croatia, where I specialize in operating and commissioning astronomical observatories and coordinate projects. Most of my nights are spent at the 1.0-m f/2.9 telescope at Tičan (Minor Planet Center code L01), following up freshly discovered near-Earth objects (NEOs) from the Minor Planet Center's NEO Confirmation Page and co-discovering new ones, together with Korado Korlević. On 1 July 2025 our observations made it into the discovery circular of 3I/ATLAS, only the third interstellar object ever found.

By day I'm building new software, programming robotic observatories and doing educational work. In 2026 I was also a mentor for the students of the University of Washington's study-abroad program at Višnjan, and we worked on projects developing the next fully remote robotic observatory for the University of Washington Department of Astronomy's observational astronomy course.

Outside the observing queue I'm also doing deep-sky astrophotography, from a simple DSLR + lens setup to high-integration images with a robotic observatory.

Read more about my research, fly through a 3D map of every asteroid and comet I've observed, browse my publications, or see the astrophotography gallery.
