Copyright & usage FAQ

© 2026 Luka Poropat. All rights reserved. Unless credited otherwise, all text, photographs, astrophotos, figures and visualisations on this site are my work. Here is what that means in practice.

Can I use or repost your astrophotos and other images?

Not without my permission. Please don't copy, repost, print, sell, edit or include them in other work (including datasets for training AI models) unless I have agreed in writing. Each image is © Luka Poropat, Višnjan Observatory. All rights reserved.

Can I share a link to your work?

Yes, always. Linking to any page on this site, or to my posts on AstroBin, Instagram and Flickr, is welcome. Linking does not need permission; copying the image itself does.

I'm a teacher, student or journalist. Can I use an image?

Please ask. Tell me about the project, where the image will appear and whether the use is commercial. If I can say yes, I'll ask for a clear credit line ("© Luka Poropat, Višnjan Observatory") and, where possible, a link to this site.

Can I use the statistics, the 3D map or the observation data?

The underlying measurements are published by the IAU Minor Planet Center, the orbits come from NASA/JPL's Small-Body Database and the station and observer statistics from MPEC Watch. Those data are available from their original sources under their own terms. The way they are compiled and presented here (the texts, charts and the interactive map) is © Luka Poropat. You're welcome to quote individual numbers with a link to the page; for anything more, please ask.

What about the names and logos of other organisations?

Names such as NASA ADS, the Minor Planet Center, AstroBin, Instagram and Flickr belong to their respective owners and are used here only to link to their services. Višnjan Observatory's own name, logo and materials belong to the observatory.

How do I ask for permission?

Email luka@astro.hr with the image or material you'd like to use, where and for how long it will appear, whether the use is commercial, and how it will be credited.

Something here is yours, or should be credited differently?

Please email me and I'll correct it promptly.
